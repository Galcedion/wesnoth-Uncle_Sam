# Change Log

Version changelog for Uncle Sam

## 1.0 - 18.11.2026

### Added

#### Capabilities

- Equal starting gold and income for all players throughout the game
- Unit limit (see known issues)
- Optional visual text indication of mod actions

#### Settings

- Starting gold
- Gold income per cycle
- Cycle frequency
- Unit limit
- Ingame messages of mod actions

#### Localization

- German (de)

### Known Issues

- Units Limit: the limit is not recalculated after a unit of the current player dies
- Units Limit: the limit is only enforced after being exceeded
